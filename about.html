About us

Making property services easier to reach

This app was created in August 2026 to make property-related services easier to access and more convenient for everyday people. It was designed and built by Angel Waigi using Visual Studio Code, Tailwind CSS, and JavaScript to create a user-friendly experience for booking and managing services.

House hunting

This website helps individuals connect with agents in the area they want to live in. It makes it easier to find suitable homes without spending long hours searching or traveling unnecessarily.

Land surveying

This service helps people assess a piece of land before building, farming, investing, or starting a new project. It allows users to find professionals who can measure and map land accurately.

Property valuation

Property valuation helps determine the economic value of land or property. This service supports informed decisions for buying, selling, or investing in real estate.
